# Changelog

All notable changes to **One Minute of Light** are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## 2026-10-23

### Added

- `thirty-seconds-of-light.html`: the thirty-second study that came first: 262,144 particles morphing through five forms in one vertex shader.
- `one-minute-of-light-v1-four-chapters.html`: four-chapter real-time film with a live WebAudio score.
- `one-minute-of-light-v2-titles-and-credits.html`: adds an opening title sequence and closing credits.
- `one-minute-of-light-v3-five-chapters.html`: expands the film to five chapters.
