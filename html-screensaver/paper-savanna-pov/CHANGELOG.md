# Changelog

All notable changes to **Paper Savanna POV** are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [1.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- `paper-savanna-pov.html`: first-person companion to [Paper Savanna](../paper-savanna/). A low flight at 108 km/h over a flat-shaded 3D origami savanna (canvas, no libraries) that banks into turns and holds about 24 m above the ground ahead. Acacias, kopjes, termite mounds, waterholes, walking giraffe and elephant herds, crane flocks at dawn and dusk, and a distant flat-topped snow peak. Same 60-second day as Paper Savanna: sky colours, sunrise/sunset, moonrise/moonset and phase that advance each loop, stars that wheel overnight, and rotating weather with 3D clouds, rain streaming past the camera and lightning. The paper flight card adds altitude, speed and bank.
- Preview params: `?t=<seconds into the day, 0-60>` and `?w=<storm|clear|cumulus|showers>`.
