# Changelog

All notable changes to **Four Billion Years** are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## 2026-10-05

### Changed

- Moved into `html-screensaver/evolution-screensaver/` as part of the repo reorganisation.

## [2.0] - 2026-08-09

### Added

- `evolution-screensaver-zoom-track.html`: extended to a six-minute seamless loop; the camera periodically picks one creature out of the crowd, descends to it and follows it until it dies or the survey moves on.

## [1.0] - 2026-08-09

### Added

- `evolution-screensaver.html`: 60-second seamless loop where cellular-automaton life blooms into increasingly complex creatures, viewed top-down under a rotating day/night sky and drifting weather.
