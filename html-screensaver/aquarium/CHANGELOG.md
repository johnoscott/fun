# Changelog

## [2.0] - 2026-10-08

### Added

- 156 cartoon animals with distinct behaviours: seven fish schools using cohesion, alignment, separation, and shark avoidance; grazing and cruising turtles; patrolling sharks; gliding rays; pulsing jellyfish; exploring octopuses; and kelp-sheltering seahorses.
- Four reef habitats: coral gardens, a tall kelp meadow, a ribbed sailing shipwreck with a torn sail and treasure chest, and an open stone cave with glowing growths.
- Seabed crabs, branching and table corals, anemones, and an instanced seagrass carpet.
- A submarine survey route past the habitats, with habitat and animal behaviour readouts.

### Changed

- Daylight still completes a full 24-hour cycle in 60 real seconds; skipping daylight no longer teleports the submarine or animals.
- Static reef geometry is batched by material to reduce rendering work; v1 remains available.

## [1.0] - 2026-10-07

### Added

- Self-contained cartoon 3D aquarium with an automatically cruising submarine viewpoint.
- A 60-second day/night cycle with rising and setting sun and moon.
- Colorful fish, sharks, rays, turtles, jellyfish, octopuses, seahorses, coral, and kelp.
- Automatic underwater weather: clear currents, bubble showers, snowfall, and electric squalls.
- Fullscreen, pause, weather selection, time skipping, and controls that fade when idle.
