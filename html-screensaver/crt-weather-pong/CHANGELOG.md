# Changelog

All notable changes to **CRT Weather Pong** are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## 2026-11-11

### Added

- `crt-weather-pong.html`: A black-and-white Pong rally on a simulated CRT, played through a scripted day of fog, sun, cumulus and a thunderstorm. The score is the clock, and 24 hours pass every 60 seconds. Temperature sets the ball's pace, wind pushes it, rain drags it down, and fog blurs the paddles' aim.
- Added a UTF-8 charset and viewport meta so the degree sign renders when served over HTTP.
