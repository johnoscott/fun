# Changelog

All notable changes to **Paper Savanna Rainbow** are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [1.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- `paper-savanna-rainbow.html`: a single-file origami savanna flyover that runs a full day in a seamless sixty-second loop. Layered paper mountains, acacia and baobab trees, kopjes, a watering hole and parading wildlife; a faceted sun and moon with an almanac for sunrise, sunset, moonrise and moonset; and a weather arc that moves from dawn mist and heat shimmer into a monsoon storm with rain, lightning and a folded-paper rainbow. Includes an auto-hiding clock card, scrubbable timeline, pause and 1×/2×/4× speed controls, and full-screen on double-click. No libraries or external assets.
