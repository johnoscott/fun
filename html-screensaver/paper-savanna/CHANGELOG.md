# Changelog

All notable changes to **Paper Savanna** are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [1.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- `paper-savanna.html`: origami flyover of the Maasai Mara where a full day passes every 60 seconds, on a loop. Parallax paper hills, acacias, giraffes, elephants and crane flocks; a faceted sun and moon with real sunrise/sunset times and moonrise/moonset that shift with the lunar phase each loop; weather that rotates daily between thunderstorm, clear, scattered cumulus and showers; and a folded-paper almanac card that hides when idle.
