# Changelog

All notable changes to **Animated Bar Chart** are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## 2026-11-12

### Added

- `src/`: A 10-second animated bar chart: axes draw in, five bars of made-up values grow with springy easing, and their labels count up. Made with [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev/) at 1280×720 and 30 fps.
- `bar-chart.html`: the composition bundled with `@remotion/player` into one self-contained page that plays live in the browser, looping, with playback controls.
