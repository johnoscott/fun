# Changelog

All notable changes to **Countdown** are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## 2026-11-12

### Added

- `src/`: A 10-second kinetic countdown: five to one, each number entering a different way behind a circular wipe and progress ring, then a burst of “Go!”. Made with [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev/) at 1280×720 and 30 fps.
- `countdown.html`: the composition bundled with `@remotion/player` into one self-contained page that plays live in the browser, looping, with playback controls.
