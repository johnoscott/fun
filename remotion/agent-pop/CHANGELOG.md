# Changelog

All notable changes to **Agent Pop** are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## 2026-11-12

### Added

- `src/`: A 10-second title card: the letters of “Agent Pop” spring in one by one over pulsing rings, with an underline and tagline, then fade out. Made with [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev/) at 1280×720 and 30 fps.
- `agent-pop.html`: the composition bundled with `@remotion/player` into one self-contained page that plays live in the browser, looping, with playback controls.
